# Ethics, Consent & Licensing

## 1. Consent Record
- **Subject**: *The Frequency of Memory* (የማስታወሻ ሞገድ) portrays "Ato Berhanu," a composite archetype inspired by the real generational radio and watch repair technicians of Ethiopia (such as the artisans of Piazza and Mercato).
- **Synthetic Identity**: To respect family privacy, all visual depictions and voices are 100% synthetically generated and do not replicate or clone the likeness, photo, or private audio of a living person without documented permission.
- **Cultural Integrity**: Scripting and phrasing in Amharic have been designed respectfully, avoiding stereotypes, with consultations from native speakers to preserve authentic terminology (e.g., *ራዲዮ*, *ሽቦ*, *ሞገድ*, *ትዝታ*).

## 2. Platform AI Disclosure & Labelling Plan
- **Mandatory End Card**: The final video file contains a clear 2.5-second concluding end card:
  > *"Created with Artificial Intelligence as part of 10 Academy's Heritage Short Film Project."*
- **Social Media Metadata**: When shared on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or LinkedIn, the native platform "Altered or Synthetic Content" label will be explicitly toggled on.
- **Provenance**: SynthID and C2PA metadata will be preserved in all generated outputs where supported.
